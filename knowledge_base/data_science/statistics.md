# Statistical Inference & Hypothesis Testing

## Fundamentals of Statistical Testing
Hypothesis testing evaluates population claims using sample data:
- **Null Hypothesis (H0)**: Statement of no effect or no difference.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (H1)**: Statement of expected effect.
- **p-value**: Probability of obtaining test results at least as extreme as observed results, assuming H0 is true.
- **Significance Level (Alpha)**: Threshold (typically 0.05) for rejecting H0.

## Error Types & Parametric vs Non-Parametric Tests
- **Type I Error (Alpha)**: False Positive (Rejecting true H0).
- **Type II Error (Beta)**: False Negative (Failing to reject false H0).
- **Power**: `1 - Beta` - Probability of correctly rejecting false H0.

Common tests:
- **Two-Sample t-test**: Compares means of two continuous populations.
- **ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)**: Compares means across three or more groups.
- **Chi-Square Test**: Tests independence between categorical variables.
- **Mann-Whitney U Test**: Non-parametric alternative to independent two-sample t-test.
